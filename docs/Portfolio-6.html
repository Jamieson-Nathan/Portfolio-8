Portfolio 6: Exploratory Analyses

The purpose of these analyses was to explore whether any of the additional variables measured (such as depression, intimacy, confidence) would moderate the relationship between self- and partner-reports of posttratumatic growth. For example, we thought that higher ‘thoughtfulness’ in response may lead to higher corroboration, i.e. a higher trait-level agreement.I hate to spoil it for you, but all moderators were null, thus why the main analysis part got sheered down a little. Nonetheless, lets see if its easy to run these in R!
